👀 TL;DR
Theme: Help people understand what their money is likely to do next.

Who can join: Anyone in the Investec Developer Community, solo or as a team.

Goal: Use Investec transaction and account data to forecast future balances, spot recurring payments and surface financial risks before they happen.

Skill level: Beginner-friendly, with plenty of room for ambitious AI-powered builds.

What counts: A working build, public repository, clear README and meaningful use of the Investec API.

Rewards: Investec Developer swag, community recognition and extra visibility for standout builds.

🔮 The Challenge
Build something that answers one deceptively simple question:

What’s likely to happen to my money next?

Most banking tools are very good at showing the past. This challenge is about looking forward.

Using Investec transaction and account data, create a tool that helps users:

forecast future balances

detect recurring payments, subscriptions and debit orders

understand the likely impact of upcoming payments

surface cashflow risks before they become problems

ask financial questions in plain language

make smarter, better-informed money decisions

The idea comes directly from recurring Private Banking client feedback asking for better visibility into upcoming debit orders, future cashflow and proactive financial guidance.

Think useful first. Fancy second. A small tool that gives one genuinely helpful warning beats a crystal ball with a loading spinner.

🛠️ Choose Your Build Level
There is no single right way to tackle this bounty. Start small, then add more intelligence if time and caffeine allow.

🌱 Starter | See What’s Coming
Your build should include:

Investec API integration

transaction analysis

detection of recurring payments or debit orders

a basic future-balance projection

Example output:

You have R4,500 available today. Based on known recurring payments, you’ll likely have R1,200 remaining before payday.

🚀 Intermediate | Help Me Plan
Include everything in the Starter level, plus one or more of the following:

spending categorisation

an upcoming-payment timeline

notifications or warnings

future cashflow visualisation

“Can I afford this?” calculations

Example output:

Buying this TV today will leave you below your expected debit-order requirements in nine days.

🧠 Advanced | Chat With Future You
Include everything above, then layer in more advanced capabilities such as:

an AI-powered financial assistant

natural-language questions

scenario planning

personalised recommendations

explainable risk indicators

automated savings suggestions

Example output:

You can afford the flight if you reduce restaurant spending for the next two weeks.

AI is optional. If you use it, explain what it does, what data it uses, its limitations and where a human should remain in control.
